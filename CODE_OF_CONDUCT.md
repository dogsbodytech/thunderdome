# Code of Conduct

We want the Thunderdome project to be a welcoming and enjoyable place for everyone to contribute, both online and at events.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, friendly and patient.
- Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Give constructive feedback and assume good intent.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact a Dogsbody Technology maintainer privately using the contact details on the [Dogsbody Technology website](https://www.dogsbody.com/). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when they reasonably believe this Code of Conduct has been breached.

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository and to project-related interactions at events or in other public spaces.
